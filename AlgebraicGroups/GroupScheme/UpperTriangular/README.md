# Upper-triangular closed subgroup scheme of GL

Import `AlgebraicGroups.GroupScheme.UpperTriangular` for the finite-type closed
upper-triangular group subscheme of GL over any commutative ring `K`. Its
coordinate algebra is in `AlgebraicGroups.Algebra.UpperTriangularCoordinateRing`;
the aggregate `AlgebraicGroups` imports the group-scheme producer. See the
[scheme producer](../UpperTriangular.lean), [coordinate producer](../../Algebra/UpperTriangularCoordinateRing.lean),
[native upper-triangular group](../../GroupTheory/UpperTriangular/README.md),
[ordinary-import client](../../../AlgebraicGroupsTest/UpperTriangularScheme.lean),
and [build guidance](../../../docs/BUILDING.md).

For any finite linearly ordered index type `n`, including `Fin 0`, and any
commutative `K`-algebra `R`, including zero and nonreduced rings, these results
require no field, flatness, nontriviality, reducedness or injectivity premise.

## Construction and API

`UpperTriangularCoordinateRing.CoordinateRing K n` is the quotient of the
**determinant-localized** GL coordinate ring by the ideal generated by the
entries `matrix K n i j` for `j < i`. Its diagonal and strictly upper entries
are not set to one or zero. `quotient`, `quotient_lower`, `universal_apply`,
`universal_diagUnit` and `universal_detInverse` read back the quotient and the
invertible universal GL matrix. The determinant-inverse relation says that
the image of the GL determinant inverse times the product of the diagonal
entries is one; it does not set the determinant inverse itself equal to one.

`ideal_isHopfIdeal` gives a commutative Hopf quotient and `finiteType` descends
from GL. The coproduct argument kills every lower-entry generator **in the
quotient tensor product** by splitting the sum at `k < i`, without assuming
injectivity or flatness of the tensor factors. The antipode argument uses the
inverse of the universal invertible GL matrix and closure of the native
upper-triangular subgroup under inverse.

`AlgebraicGeometry.upperTriangularGroupUnderlyingScheme K n` is the resulting
affine finite-type scheme over `Spec K`, and `upperTriangularGroupScheme K n`
is its group object. `upperTriangularInclusion K n` is a genuine group-scheme
morphism into `generalLinearGroupScheme K n`; its underlying scheme map is
a closed immersion (`upperTriangularInclusion_isClosedImmersion`) from the
surjective quotient, not merely a pointwise inclusion.

For every commutative `K`-algebra `R`, `upperTriangularToGL` and
`upperTriangularFromGL` give inverse algebra-map classifications of native
`Matrix.UpperTriangularGroup n R`. The multiplicative versions are
`upperTriangularGroupMulEquivAlgHom K n R` via convolution and
`upperTriangularGroupMulEquivPoints K n R` via `Spec.mapMulEquiv`.
`upperTriangularInclusion_point` identifies the inclusion with the ambient
native GL inclusion. `upperTriangularGroupFunctor`,
`upperTriangularGroupPointsFunctor` and `upperTriangularGroupPointsIso`
give a group-valued natural isomorphism over `CommAlgCat K`. Both
`upperTriangularToGL_natural` and `upperTriangularFromGL_natural` hold under
**every fixed-base algebra map**, including noninjective maps; they do not
claim arbitrary-base scheme change.

The separate ordinary-import client checks the general triangles, products,
inclusion and naturality; `Fin 0`/`Fin 1`, genuine `Fin 2` shear and two
independent diagonal units, `ZMod 1` in `Fin 0` and `Fin 2`, a nonzero
square-zero `DualNumber.eps`, and a proved noninjective `ℤ →ₐ[ℤ] ZMod 1`.
The [native client](../../../AlgebraicGroupsTest/UpperTriangular.lean)
separately checks the U-first native semidirect decomposition. No represented
semidirect splitting, diagonal projection/section, product-of-`Gₘ` scheme,
smoothness, dimension, arbitrary-base change or source-specific coverage is
claimed here.
