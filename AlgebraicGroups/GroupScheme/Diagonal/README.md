# Native diagonal closed group scheme

Import `AlgebraicGroups.GroupScheme.Diagonal` for the diagonal subgroup of the
native matrix general linear group and its affine group scheme over any
commutative base ring `K`. The index type `ι` is finite with decidable equality;
it need not be ordered or inhabited. Every coefficient ring `R` is commutative
and, for scheme-valued points, a `K`-algebra. Neither `K` nor `R` must be
reduced, nonzero, a field or a domain.

The lower-level `AlgebraicGroups.GroupTheory.Diagonal` defines
`Matrix.DiagonalGroup ι R` as the literal subgroup of
`Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup ι R` whose entries vanish for **all** `i ≠ j`.
`Matrix.DiagonalGroup.inclusion` embeds it in native GL and
`Matrix.DiagonalGroup.unitsEquiv` identifies it multiplicatively with
`ι → Rˣ`. Its inverse sends a tuple of *units* to the native diagonal matrix;
`Matrix.DiagonalGroup.map` changes coefficients entrywise, and
`Matrix.DiagonalGroup.unitsEquiv_map` identifies its action with pointwise
`Units.map`. Invertibility of a matrix means its determinant is a **unit**;
nonzero determinant alone does not suffice over a general ring.

`DiagonalCoordinateRing.CoordinateRing K ι` is the quotient of the
determinant-localized GL coordinate algebra by the ideal generated by every
off-diagonal universal entry. The ideal is a Hopf ideal: the coproduct relation
vanishes after mapping *both* factors to the quotient; the antipode relation
uses inverse closure of the actual native diagonal matrix unit, without
flatness or field-point detection. The quotient universal entries on the
diagonal are units. `universal_detInverse` reads the inverse-determinant
coordinate as inverse to their finite product. The quotient is finite type
over `K`.

`AlgebraicGeometry.diagonalGroupScheme K ι` is the represented finite-type
affine group object over `Spec K`; `diagonalInclusion K ι` is its closed
group-scheme morphism into the existing GL group scheme, with
`diagonalInclusion_isClosedImmersion` witnessing its geometric property.
`diagonalGroupMulEquivAlgHom` and `diagonalGroupMulEquivPoints` give native
diagonal units as all-algebra quotient and affine-scheme points;
`diagonalGroupUnitsEquivPoints` composes with the unit-tuple identification.
`diagonalGroupPointsIso` is the group-valued natural isomorphism on every
`CommAlgCat K` algebra. Readbacks include
`diagonalGroupMulEquivAlgHom_entry`,
`diagonalGroupMulEquivAlgHom_detInverse`, and
`diagonalInclusion_point` (the closed inclusion is native GL inclusion on
points); `diagonalToGL_natural` and `diagonalFromGL_natural` support arbitrary
coefficient `K`-algebra maps. This is naturality in test algebras **at fixed
base**; it is not a base-change theorem.

The ordinary-import client `AlgebraicGroupsTest.Diagonal` checks empty index
tuples (`Fin 0`), one unit/G_m **points** (`Fin 1`), independent pair entries
(`Fin 2`), the zero ring `ZMod 1`, and the nonreduced dual-number algebra
`DualNumber ℤ` with the nonzero square-zero parameter `ε` and diagonal unit
`1 + ε`, inverse `1 - ε`. These tests are not restricted to geometric points.


A finite-product `Gₘ` **scheme** isomorphism is a distinct result in the
[diagonal-product guide](../DiagonalProduct/README.md), not a consequence
of the unit-tuple equivalence on points alone.
